Mauritius Airport Transfer - Mauritius Transport

Our airport transfers and welcome services from the SSR International Airport of Mauritius, or to and from any location on the island can be booked online. e offer private transfers for individuals, families and groups with the option of our Signature Meet and Assist service (The Rainbow IO ©).

The vehicles range from an accredited taxi service, mini bus, coach or VIP luxury transfer options and helicopter, to suit your needs and budget, whether you are travelling alone, with family, in a group or on a business trip.

Individual airport transfers can be booked at the same time as booking accommodation or at a later stage, in case the traveller does not have the flight details to hand.

All drivers and guest relations staff are well trained to assist, guide and ensure that our guests are well received at both the airport and at the accommodation booked.

The vehicles available for Group Transfers are a combination of late model charter vans, mini-buses, and 24 to 54 seater coaches, which allow for schedule flexibility. We cannot guarantee what type of vehicle will be provided, as this is dictated by the size of the group.

We also offer car rentals in the following categories: compact, luxury sedan, and limousine with private chauffer.
