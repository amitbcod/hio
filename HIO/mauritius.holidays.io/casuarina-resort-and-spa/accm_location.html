Casuarina Resort & Spa is located in the picturesque Trou aux Biches on the North West Coast of Mauritius. Just 5 km from the vibrant Grand Bay and 18 km from Port Louis, the island's capital, this resort offers a perfect blend of convenience and tropical charm for an unforgettable getaway.

Location Description:

Area: Trou aux Biches, North West of Mauritius

Hotel Address: Coastal Road, Trou aux Biches

Distance from airport: 58km, 70 minutes

Distance from Port-Louis: 18km, 23 minutes
