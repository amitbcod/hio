Activities in Mauritius

Rainbow Activities

Explore the beauty of Mauritius with a range of sustainable activities. Every excursion, from exploring tranquil beaches and local markets to trekking towering hills and diving into crystal-clear waters, is designed with the preservation of the island in mind. Rainbow Tours will immerse you in the island's culture, history, and breathtaking scenery, while health activities will revitalise both your body and soul. Enjoy meaningful activities that connect you to Mauritius while helping to ensure its long-term viability.
